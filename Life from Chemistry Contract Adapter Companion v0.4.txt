2. COMMON EVIDENCE-STATE GRAMMAR
AMC and OCTT use three evidence states:
DISCHARGED
The declared burden has adequate supporting evidence under the current contract.
VIOLATED
The declared burden has adequate evidence showing that the burden fails.
OPEN
The burden has not been adequately discharged or violated.
OPEN does not mean FALSE.
OPEN does not mean FAILED.
A missing or inadequate assay remains OPEN unless the contract separately warrants a violation.
