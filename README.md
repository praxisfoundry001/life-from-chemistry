Life from Chemistry

An Abiogenesis Experiment for Autonomous Maintenance

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22848429

This repository contains one coordinated Structural Flow publication package centered on the scientific artifact Life from Chemistry: An Abiogenesis Experiment for Autonomous Maintenance.

The work asks a deliberately narrow origin-of-life question:

When the material that initially carried an organized chemistry is lost, diluted, degraded, or replaced, what continues—and does the continuing organization itself carry the maintenance relations required for its continued competence?

The experiment does not define Life by persistence, reproduction, growth, chemistry that merely continues, or material turnover alone.

Three layers that must remain separate

The public architecture contains three distinct scientific jobs:

OCTT asks what organizational continuity relation is supported through load-bearing material turnover.

AMC asks whether the declared system carries an autonomous-maintenance organization at the declared system, environment, level, scope, horizon, boundary, process set, and control set.

Life translation asks whether an empirically admitted AMC object lawfully realizes the scoped Structural Flow Life object.

The permitted direction is:

OCTT evidence → AMC adjudication → separate Life translation

No reverse shortcut is installed.

OCTT does not define AMC.

AMC does not define Life.

Contract closure does not supply empirical entry.

The machine package also contains prospective result branches. Those branches specify in advance what future evidence would license stronger conclusions. They do not assert that those results have occurred.

OCTT result grammar

OCTT distinguishes:

C1 — continuous bounded carry

C2 — daughter-lineage carry

C3 — de novo nucleation / new-carrier production

C4 — dissolution / reconstitution

C5 — population propagation

Indeterminate

C3, C4, and C5 are scientifically meaningful classified outcomes. They are not automatically treated as failed experiments.

A C1 or C2 continuity result does not by itself establish autonomous maintenance.

AMC admission

AMC requires independent burdens concerning:

explicit system/environment/level/scope/horizon/boundary/process/control pins;

control efficacy;

endogeneity;

mutual integration;

regeneration;

continued organizational competence;

assay adequacy.

A positive continuity result cannot supply an AMC admission by itself.

Likewise, an inadequate assay or unresolved burden remains OPEN rather than being converted into evidence that autonomous maintenance is absent.

Life translation

Only after AMC is empirically admitted does the public adapter ask whether that admitted organization realizes the scoped Structural Flow Life object.

The machine job is:

maintenance-bearing continuation

The installed weakest shape is:

self-defending boundary regime

The selected capacity is:

viable continuation

The translation is allowed to fail.

A chemistry may satisfy AMC while remaining too weak, too broadly distributed, or incorrectly scoped to realize the Life object. Such a result would preserve the autonomous-maintenance finding while leaving the Life claim open.

What this work does not claim

This release does not claim that:

Hardy's system is alive;

Matsuo's system is alive;

AMC is identical to Life;

material turnover proves strict identity;

parent-to-daughter inheritance proves strict identity;

C1 or C2 automatically establishes AMC;

C3, C4, or C5 is a failed experiment;

differentiated controls are universally necessary;

the proposed experiment has already been performed;

machine cleanliness establishes empirical truth;

or these model systems reconstruct the historical path of terrestrial abiogenesis.
